Indoor Air Science Notes publishes educational articles about indoor air quality. It is sponsored by [Ducts On Call](https://ductsoncall.com), an HVAC and duct cleaning company.

**How articles are made:** Articles are drafted with AI assistance from public health agencies (EPA, CDC, WHO, NIOSH) and peer-reviewed literature, then checked against the cited sources. Claims go only as far as the evidence supports.

**Not medical advice.** For health concerns, consult a physician. For suspected building contamination, consult a qualified professional.

**Corrections:** If you spot an error, contact Ducts On Call through [ductsoncall.com](https://ductsoncall.com).
